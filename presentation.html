Phase 2 — Research Questions

6 Questions. Real Answers.
No model required.

RQ1 — Funding
6% → 19%
Acquisition rate rises with seed amount. Sweet spot: $500K–$2M
RQ2 — Industry
2–3×
Semiconductors & Security have 2–3× the average acquisition rate. Software: 11%, Biotech: 5.2%
RQ3 — Geography
15% vs 3%
Silicon Valley startups acquired at 15%. Non-USA: just 3%. Location is destiny.
RQ4 — Angel Funding
+7pp
Angel-backed startups have 7 percentage points higher acquisition rate. Grants: weaker effect.
RQ5 — Speed
11% acq.
Startups that raised within 3 months of founding have the highest acquisition rate. Speed = signal.
RQ6 — Founding Era
⚠ Bias
Pre-2000 looks best — survivorship bias. 2012+ looks worst — they're too young to be acquired yet.
Important: Correlation ≠ Causation. These are patterns in historical data. We cannot say seed funding causes acquisition. We predict what we observe, with full awareness of survivorship and immaturity biases.

The No-Leakage Guarantee

The Pipeline was fitted on training data only — medians, target-encoding means, and scalers are computed from training rows, then applied identically to validation and test. The test set was never examined until the final evaluation.

Target Encoding — Why Not One-Hot?

753 markets + 115 countries = ~870 dummy columns. Target encoding collapses each category to just 2 informative numbers: mean acquisition rate & mean closure rate.

Key Lessons

What we'd tell
every data scientist.

Feature Engineering Beats Model Choice

The 39 → 46 feature transformation — log-transforms, geo clusters, target encoding — mattered far more than which algorithm we used. Data quality first, model second.

Imbalance Follows You Everywhere

Resampling in notebook 04 was just the beginning. Every downstream decision — metrics, thresholds, per-class F1 — all had to account for the 15:1 majority/minority ratio.

Correlation ≠ Causation

We predict patterns, not mechanisms. Silicon Valley startups aren't acquired because they're in Silicon Valley — both reflect a deeper selection effect. Honest scope matters.

Vote Before You Select

No single feature selection method is the oracle. Running 8 methods and requiring ≥5/8 agreement produced a robust, reproducible feature set that survived correlation pruning.

The Test Set is Sacred

50 experiments, hyperparameter tuning, feature selection — all on training + validation only. The test set was opened exactly once. That's the only way final numbers mean anything.

Bias Awareness is Non-Negotiable

Survivorship bias, maturity bias, causality limitations — we documented every one. A model without stated limitations is a model you can't trust.
